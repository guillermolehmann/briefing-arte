That quote up top is Costantini's, and here is why he said it. The Malba confirmed it bought the Daros Latinamerica collection, one thousand two hundred and thirty three works by one hundred and seventeen artists. Swiss collector Ruth Schmidheiny had put it together in Zurich starting in the year two thousand, and it sat unseen after she died in two thousand and nineteen. It holds pieces by names we have already been following on this show, like Gego and Hélio Oiticica. The purchase doubles the museum's permanent collection in one move, bringing it to roughly three thousand works, and adds seventy five artists the Malba never had on its walls before. The price was not made public. Costantini's move lands right before the museum's twenty fifth anniversary, celebrated next week with a gala on Thursday the seventeenth, free admission on Saturday the nineteenth, Sunday the twentieth and Monday the twenty first, and the South American debut of Viva Frida, a show with more than three hundred and sixty pieces by Frida Kahlo. For the Latin American collector circuit you follow closely, this is the kind of institutional announcement that later shows up in the resale price of artists who enter a collection that size.
